# Author Guidelines

## 1. Types of Manuscripts

JITeCS publishes high-quality research in the field of information technology, computer science, and related disciplines. The journal accepts the following types of manuscripts:

Original Research Articles: Manuscripts presenting novel contributions, innovative approaches, or significant research findings supported by rigorous methodology.
Ongoing Research Articles: Manuscripts reporting work-in-progress or preliminary results with clear research direction and potential impact.
Review/Survey Articles: Manuscripts providing critical and comprehensive reviews of existing studies, highlighting research gaps and future directions.

## 2. Manuscript Preparation

Authors must ensure that manuscripts:

- Are original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere.
- Are written in clear, concise, and academically appropriate English.
- Follow a standard scientific structure (e.g., Abstract, Keywords, Introduction, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion).
- Use the official MS Word template available at ./Template\ JITeCS\ v0.3.docx.
- Are prepared using Times New Roman, 10 pt.
- Do not exceed: 12 pages for original research articles, 10 pages for ongoing research articles, and 16 pages for literature review articles, following the journal's formatting guidelines.

# Additional Requirements

### Abstract and Keywords
- The abstract should systematically present the research background, objectives, methodology, results, and conclusions.
- It must not exceed 200 words.
- It must clearly highlight the key findings and the main contributions of the study.
- It must contain 3 to 5 keywords or key phrases.

### Figures and Tables

- All figures and tables must be clearly presented, properly numbered, and cited in the text.
- Figure captions must be placed below the figure, while table captions should be placed above the table.
- Ensure that all text, symbols, and details within figures are clearly legible.
- Images should have a minimum resolution of 300 dpi.
- All figures and tables must follow the journal template.

### Mathematical Expressions and Symbols
- Mathematical equations should be clearly presented, centered, and placed on a separate line.
- Equations must be numbered consecutively (e.g., (1), (2)) and aligned to the right margin.
- All symbols and variables must be clearly defined in the text.
- Equations should be written consistently and integrated into the sentence structure when appropriate.
- Authors are encouraged to use standard equation editors (e.g., MS Equation Editor or MathType).

### Algorithms, Pseudocode, and Program Code
- Only essential parts relevant to the discussion should be included.
- Algorithms and pseudocode must be clearly presented and easy to read.
- Each algorithm or code snippet must be titled and numbered, and referenced in the text.
- Authors are encouraged to use a monospaced font (e.g., Courier New).
- Proper comments should be included where necessary.
- Full implementations may be provided as supplementary material or external repositories.

### Supplementary Materials
- Authors are encouraged to provide supplementary materials to support transparency, reproducibility, and open science practices.
- These may include datasets, source code, experimental results, or additional documentation.
- Such materials should be deposited in open-access repositories (e.g., Mendeley Data, Zenodo, OSF, or other publicly accessible repositories), with links provided in the manuscript.

### Reference Style
All manuscripts must follow the IEEE referencing style:

- References are numbered sequentially in the order of citation.
- In-text citations must use square brackets (e.g., [1], [2]).
- The reference list must follow IEEE standards.

Authors are responsible for ensuring the accuracy and completeness of all references.

### Language Quality
- Authors are encouraged to ensure professional language editing prior to submission.
- Authors may be requested to revise and proofread their manuscripts before publication.
- Manuscripts must use inclusive and respectful language, avoiding bias related to age, gender, race, ethnicity, culture, sexual orientation, disability, or health condition.


## 3. Submission and Peer-Review Process

- Submission of a manuscript to JITeCS implies that the manuscript has not been previously published or submitted elsewhere.
- One corresponding author must be designated (for multi-author submissions) to handle the manuscript submission and provide any additional information requested by the editorial team.
- All communications will be conducted exclusively through the corresponding author via the OJS platform.
- All manuscripts must be submitted through the journal's online submission system.
- Each manuscript is assigned to a Section Editor to conduct a preliminary review. Eligible manuscripts will be sent to selected reviewers to undergo a double-blind peer-review process to ensure the anonymity of authors and reviewers.
- The initial review decision is typically provided within 4-8 weeks. The overall publication process, including revisions, may take approximately 2-4 months, depending on the extent of revisions required.
- Manuscripts found to be simultaneously submitted to or published in other venues during the review process will be automatically rejected. In cases of confirmed ethical misconduct, the journal reserves the right to take further actions in accordance with publication ethics guidelines.

## 4. Revision and Final Decision

- Authors may be required to revise their manuscripts based on reviewers' comments. Revised manuscripts must be submitted within 2 weeks of the revision request.
- Failure to submit the revised manuscript within the specified timeframe may lead to the withdrawal or rejection of the manuscript. The editorial office may issue reminder notifications; however, lack of response after repeated reminders will be considered as a withdrawal from the submission process.
- Revisions must adequately address all reviewers' comments. Manuscripts that fail to demonstrate satisfactory revisions may be rejected.
- The final decision regarding acceptance or rejection is made by the editorial board, based on the reviewers' recommendations and the overall quality and contribution of the manuscript.

## 5. Final Manuscript Preparation

-Prior to publication, authors are required to ensure that their manuscripts fully comply with the journal's formatting and layout requirements.
-Accepted manuscripts may undergo final copyediting and formatting. Authors will be asked to review and approve the final proofs before publication.
-If applicable, the Article Processing Charge (APC) must be settled prior to publication. For detailed information, please refer to the journal's APC page: Article Processing Charge (APC).
